About us

Rahi Da Fey is a guitar player who plays in several styles of music including rock, metal, blues, jazz, neoclassical and more. His unique style has been described as shred meets blues with some progressive elements.

He started playing guitar at the age of 16 and has been playing ever since. He has tried many ideas from different genres in a very diversified way. He has worked with many bands and has released nine albums as an unsigned artist. His most recent album, Huge city, Tenor, was released on April 14th, 2014.

After he started learning guitar, he quickly became skilled enough to play guitars, basses and keys. He plays other instruments such as piano and bass, but specializes in guitar synth.

I love to play different kinds of music styles like blues, rock, metal and even some experimental stuff that has not been named yet. Coming from a small town, I was inspired by the music with my friends. I started playing guitar and completed many songs and books. I have released nine albums as an unsigned artist. This music is available in various platforms.

He has been working with many bands over the years and has played numerous gigs. But nowadays he spends most of his time recording at studios. He plays music from all over the world in many different styles of music, from rock to jazz to blues to metal. He currently lives in Dhaka, Bangladesh where he continues his work in studio as well.
