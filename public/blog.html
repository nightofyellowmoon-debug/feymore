Weary Sphere | Chapter #2 | 14.04.2024

Full moon, alone

In a blue full moon, alone

The night of shivering wind drift away whistling

And the pain and exhaustion drawn into eyes

Confused in the dark shadow, wayward in the illusion of the way

Staring at the deep blue with two eyes

One little, fade, innocent face,

Or blue dream of the street with the shadow of amber light

This sky full of stars is like scars of a night covered with mist.

And the lost tune might drift into this shadowscape street,

The heart of the edge of illusion covered with silver mist,

Silent breaks when a nocturnal bird calls

like dew drops in the soft, wet grass.

Staring at the deep blue with two eyes

One little, fade, innocent face,

Or blue dream of the street with the shadow of amber light

This sky full of stars is like scars of a night covered with mist.

Night in a far away street, cold and alone

In a blue crescent with alone, bare alone moon,

Kiss of the light of amber at the grey black street

In a sleeping city of silent night, alone.

Staring at the deep blue with two eyes

One little, fade, innocent face

Or blue dream of the street with the shadow of amber light

This sky full of stars is like scars of a night covered with mist.

- Rahi

Edge of illusion

This haze wet bloodred dusk

And the exotic bird floating matching the wings

This shadowbound soft walk

This loved city in evening's dress

This shadowbound green edges

Bloodred colorscheme venting the trees

Is this all just illusion?

When the red sun set with

In the crowd of peoples coming home with cheers

Silver moon rises in the vent of

The tree shedding leaves

This street with the shade of amber light

Calm, Dreambound

With the game between light and dark

starts the quiet night

This dew wet soft sky

All is just illusion. Just illusion.
